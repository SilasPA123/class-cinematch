How the match works

With only four films each, identical lists are rare. So every pair of people is compared on several things at once, and each one that overlaps adds to the score and to the explanation you see.

1

Same films

The strongest signal. Every film you both picked counts the most.

2

Same directors

Different films by the same director still say a lot about taste.

3

Genres and themes

How much your genres and TMDB keywords overlap, as a share of everything on both lists.

4

Era, place, cast

Shared decades, countries, languages and actors add smaller amounts.

The points add up and are turned into a percentage that rises quickly at first and then levels off, so a few strong overlaps already make a high match. The code is in match.js.
